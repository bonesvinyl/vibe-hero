# Product
## Register
product
## Users
Desktop rhythm-game players organizing YouTube recordings and local music before a play session.
## Product Purpose
Prepare reusable charts, find a song quickly, play along with its video, and track personal performance.
## Brand Personality
Energetic, musical, playful. Preserve the user's Guitar Hero-inspired identity and readable small typography.
## Anti-references
The original library's giant repeated cards, endless mixed song/score lists, and ornamental fonts in small controls.
## Design Principles
- Songs are the primary objects; scores belong to their exact recording.
- Preserve existing charts and personal history across upgrades.
- Offer keyboard alternatives to drag-and-drop organization.
- Make saved, preparing, empty, and failed states explicit.
## Accessibility & Inclusion
Readable contrast, keyboard controls, labeled forms, reduced motion, and layouts that adapt to narrow windows follow existing app conventions.
