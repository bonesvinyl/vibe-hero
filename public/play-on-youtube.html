VIBE HERO / YOUTUBE MODE

Keep the music behind the frets.

This local extension puts Vibe Hero on YouTube’s watch page. It uses the normal YouTube player and its playback clock, avoiding the separate embedded player.

  1. In Chrome, Edge, or Brave, open the browser’s Extensions page. Enable Developer mode, choose Load unpacked, and select the dist-extension folder in your Vibe Hero project. If you received a ZIP, unzip it first and select the folder containing manifest.json.
  2. Open your song on YouTube in that browser. From the Extensions menu, choose Vibe Hero — Play on YouTube, then Play on this video.
  3. Set BPM and the first beat, then press Play from beginning. Use A S D F G for frets, Enter to pause, Space for star power, and Escape to exit. Strum mode uses the up/down arrows.

Update to 0.10.0

Replace the files in your previously loaded extension folder with the updated package, click Reload on its Extensions-page card, then refresh YouTube.

Crowd cheering and booing are back. Adjust the Crowd slider independently of the song; zero mutes it.

Gameplay upgrades

Easy uses A/S/D/F only, with fewer notes and a slower highway. Medium is gentler too. Hit notes to keep the crowd meter alive; zero means a failed set. Successful notes flare, and held notes burn through their sustain. Star power takes at least 60 seconds of song time to recharge, then lasts sixteen seconds with louder crowd audio, reverb and echo. Songs begin with a four-second countdown. Resuming after a pause gives you three seconds to get ready. Use the extension Play button to enable the audio path.

Finish or fail a run, enter a player name, and choose Save final score. Practice and failed attempts are labeled in your local history. My song library keeps your score history locally. A public leaderboard is not connected yet.

Import a prepared setlist

Open My song library and choose the song-pack JSON under Import a prepared setlist. All songs are added to the library together; they do not need another listen. Open a song on YouTube and click Play. Importing replaces any chart already saved for the same video.

Prepare quietly

Open the song on YouTube, click the extension icon, then Prepare quietly in background. You can switch tabs while preparation runs silently. Keep the song tab and browser open. Open My song library from the extension popup for progress, cancellation and saved charts. One song at a time; medium difficulty for background preparation.

The updated extension needs storage, tab capture and offscreen permissions. If your browser asks when you reload it, these enable local chart saves and quiet preparation.

Manual sharing fallback

Click Listen & build chart, choose this exact YouTube tab, and enable Share tab audio. Let the song play once at normal speed without seeking, keeping the tab foreground. Then replay the audio chart. BPM is estimated automatically. Completed charts save automatically. Save chart displays a confirmation, and reopening the same video restores the saved chart. Download JSON backup is a separate optional file export. This follows the full mix; it cannot yet isolate guitar from vocals or drums.

Hold colored frets through their tails. Harder difficulties add chord accents. The side meter shows your streak, multiplier, and bonus charge: at 100%, press Space for sixteen seconds of double points.

The app’s “Play on YouTube” link carries your BPM, first beat, difficulty, play style, and timing adjustment. Custom charts need to be imported again. This mode currently uses keyboard input, including controllers mapped to keyboard keys.

YouTube controls returns to the original player. Ads remain in the original player and gameplay waits for them. Videos must still be playable on YouTube itself; this does not remove sign-in, regional, age, or other availability requirements.

This is an unpacked prototype, not a store release. Live playback and visuals still need testing in your browser. Developers: generate the extension folder with npm run build:extension.
